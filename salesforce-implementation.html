The problem

Why do Salesforce rollouts end up as an expensive spreadsheet?

Salesforce can model almost any sales process, which is exactly why rollouts go wrong. Fields get added for every request, stages do not match how deals really move, and old automation keeps running steps nobody remembers building.

Then the data arrives messy from the old system, duplicates pile up, and reps go back to the spreadsheet they trust. Managers stop believing the forecast, and the licence bill keeps coming.

Our Salesforce implementation starts with your sales process on one page. We design the data model and permissions before configuring anything, build automation in Flow, migrate and reconcile your data, connect your other tools and train each role. If you need something Salesforce does not fit, we also build custom CRMs and Zoho setups.

Signs your Salesforce setup needs work

  • Reps keep deals in a spreadsheet next to Salesforce
  • Nobody trusts the forecast report
  • Duplicate accounts and contacts everywhere
  • Old workflow rules and Process Builder still run key steps
  • Marketing and sales numbers never match
  • Only one admin knows how anything works

FAQ

Salesforce implementation, answered

Straight answers to the questions we hear most.

Ask us on a call

What is included in your Salesforce implementation services?

Discovery and solution design, Sales Cloud or Service Cloud setup, automation in Flow, data migration and clean-up, integrations with your other tools, dashboards, and training for each role with a handover to your admin.

How long does a Salesforce implementation take?

It depends on the number of processes, users and integrations. A single sales process moving from spreadsheets is a much shorter project than several teams with integrations. The solution design gives you dates before the build starts.

What should we look for in a Salesforce implementation partner?

Ask any team you consider, us included, to show a written solution design before the build, a data migration and reconciliation plan, how they test in a sandbox, how they train each role, and who will run the org afterwards. Ask about the certifications of the people who will do the work.

Can you migrate us from HubSpot, Zoho or spreadsheets?

Yes. We map every field, run test loads into a sandbox, compare record counts and fix duplicates before the final load.

Do you write custom code in Apex?

When Flow and standard features cannot do the job, yes, with Apex and Lightning Web Components, tested and documented. We always try configuration first, because it is cheaper to maintain.

Salesforce or a custom CRM: which do we need?

Salesforce fits most sales teams that want a proven platform and a large ecosystem. If your process is unusual or licence costs do not make sense, a custom CRM can be the better choice. We build both, so we will tell you honestly which fits.

How is pricing worked out?

Our work is billed monthly on one of three plans and quoted after a free call. Salesforce licences are separate and billed by Salesforce.

How do we get started?

Book a 30-minute call. Tell us how you sell today and what is not working, and we will suggest where to start.
