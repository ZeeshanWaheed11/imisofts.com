AI Data LicensingGet paid for how your company already works.

AI labs are licensing real business records, like SOPs, workflows and project histories, to train their next models. imisofts handles the whole process for you, from the first data inventory to the day the license fee lands in your account.

The opportunity

AI already knows the internet. It doesn't know how your business runs.

The models behind today's AI tools were trained on public web pages. What they still can't see is how real companies get work done: how a quote turns into a project, how a ticket gets solved, who approves what, and what happens when something goes wrong.

That knowledge lives inside companies like yours. It sits in your SOPs, your CRM, your project boards, your helpdesk and years of internal documentation. AI labs need it to build models that can actually do business work, and they are paying companies to license it.

You are not selling your company, your clients or your records. You are licensing a cleaned, anonymized copy of how your team works, for an agreed purpose and an agreed period. You keep ownership the whole time.

  • A new revenue line.

    A license is paid to your company, and the same dataset can be licensed to more than one lab.

  • Nothing to build.

    It doesn't change how you run your business. Your team keeps working exactly as it does today.

  • No upfront cost.

    Finding out what your data could be worth costs you nothing.

What gets licensed, and what never leaves

Labs pay for the record of how work gets done, not your names or numbers.

What labs value

  • SOPs, playbooks and process documentation
  • Workflows, approval chains and handoffs
  • Ticketing, QA and support processes
  • CRM stages and pipeline workflow data
  • Project boards and project histories
  • Engineering documentation, issue triage and release workflows
  • Templates, frameworks, knowledge bases and wikis
  • Internal reports and working files
  • Team chat history, cleaned and anonymized

What stays with you

  • Names of clients, staff and partners (removed or changed)
  • Phone numbers, email addresses and social security numbers (redacted)
  • Dollar amounts and account details (redacted)
  • Anything you choose to exclude (from a single folder to a whole system)

You decide what goes in. Every file is redacted and anonymized, and can be rewritten synthetically, before a lab ever sees it. Nothing is released until you approve it.

How it works

Six steps from first call to first payment. We run every one of them with you.

  1. 01

    Data Fit Call (20 minutes, free).

    We learn how your company works and which systems you use. You don't send us a single file.

  2. 02

    Data inventory.

    We map your records system by system: what you have, how many years it covers and how it's organized. Nothing sensitive goes into this summary.

  3. 03

    Valuation.

    Your inventory is reviewed against what AI labs are buying right now, and we show you where your records fit and what drives their value.

  4. 04

    Offers and terms.

    Vetted labs make offers. We walk you through each one, the rights requested and any exclusivity, and your lawyer reviews the agreement before you sign anything.

  5. 05

    Clean, anonymize, deliver.

    Records are redacted and anonymized, and can be rewritten synthetically, before release. You approve the final dataset, and it moves only after you sign, directly to the lab.

  6. 06

    Get paid.

    The lab pays your company directly. Each lab is a separate license, so the same dataset can earn more than once.

Your team's time: one short call, a few answers about your systems, and your sign-off. We handle the rest.

What it can be worth

Licenses for established companies typically run in the six and seven figures.

$250,000 to $2,000,000

Typical license range for qualifying companies, per license. Each lab is a separate license.

What drives the value

  • Relevance.

    How closely your records match what labs are training on right now.

  • Depth.

    How many years your records cover and how many systems they span.

  • Quality.

    How complete, consistent and well documented your workflows are.

  • Rights.

    What the lab may use the data for, for how long, and whether it's exclusive.

Every company is different. Value is set by the buyer after reviewing your inventory, and no offer or payment is guaranteed.

Ownership, privacy and control

Your data, your rules, at every step.

  • You keep ownership.

    A license grants use for an agreed purpose and period. Your records stay yours.

  • You choose what's included.

    Pick systems, folders and date ranges. Leave out anything you don't want shared.

  • Anonymized before release.

    Names are changed. Phone numbers, emails, dollar amounts and social security numbers are redacted. Records can also be rewritten synthetically.

  • We never hold your raw data.

    The fit call and inventory need no files at all. After you sign, data moves directly from your company to the lab.

  • Only vetted labs.

    Every buyer is checked before an introduction, and you can ask any lab to explain its security and anonymization process in writing.

  • Nothing moves without your sign-off.

    You approve the final dataset before release, and your lawyer reviews every agreement.

FAQ

AI data licensing, answered

Straight answers to the questions owners ask first.

Ask us on a call

Why would an AI company pay for our internal records?

Public web pages don't show how real businesses make decisions, hand off work or fix problems. Labs need those examples to build AI that can do business work, and companies are the only source.

Are we selling our company data?

No. You license an anonymized copy for an agreed purpose and period. You keep ownership of your records.

Do we have to send you our data to find out if we qualify?

No. The fit call and the inventory need no files. We work from a high-level summary of your systems.

What does it cost us?

Nothing upfront. imisofts is paid only when a license closes and the payment reaches your account.

How much could our data be worth?

There's no standard price. Typical licenses for qualifying companies run from $250,000 to $2,000,000, and value depends on relevance, depth, quality and the rights requested. The buyer sets the value after reviewing your inventory.

Will anyone see our clients' or staff's information?

No. Names are changed, and contact details, dollar amounts and ID numbers are redacted before anything is released. You choose what's excluded and approve the final dataset.

Do we need to be a tech company?

No. Agencies, engineering firms, logistics companies, professional services and healthcare administration teams can all qualify.

Will this disrupt our team?

No. There's nothing to build and nothing changes in how you run the business. Your team's time is one call, a few answers and a sign-off.

Can we license the same data more than once?

Often, yes. Each lab is a separate license, so one dataset can be licensed to several labs.

Who are the buyers?

AI companies and data labs building foundation models. Every lab is vetted before an introduction, and you can review each one before you agree to anything.

How long does it take?

The fit call and inventory take days, not months. The time to an offer and payment depends on the lab, and we keep you updated at every step.

What if we decide not to go ahead?

That's fine. There's no obligation at any stage, and you keep the inventory we built together.
