FAQ

Questions buyers ask first

What exactly is for sale?

imisofts, a lead generation and AI automation agency, sold as one business: imisofts.com (the domain, brand and full website), the cold email infrastructure that generates leads for the agency, a few active monthly retainers, and the brand channels around them (YouTube, LinkedIn, social profiles, the Upwork agency profile and the affiliate income from the articles).

Is this a lead generation agency for sale with clients?

Yes. A few active monthly retainer clients come with the business, and the cold email system that wins new clients is handed over working. Client names and monthly amounts are shared by email with serious buyers.

Which accounts and channels move to the buyer?

The YouTube channel moves through a Google Brand Account ownership transfer and you become super admin of the LinkedIn company page. Other social profiles are handed over through each platform’s own admin tools where its rules allow it, and Upwork agency ownership moves through Upwork support with Upwork’s approval. Affiliate accounts stay personal, so the links in the articles move to your own partner accounts.

What is the asking price?

The price is not published. Email growth@imisofts.com and we will share it with the supporting numbers.

Can I check the traffic and the Domain Rating myself?

Yes. Domain Rating is public: look up imisofts.com in Ahrefs. Traffic and search reports are shared with serious buyers, so every number on this page can be checked before you make an offer.

How do the retainer clients transfer?

Client names, services, monthly amounts and how each relationship is handed over are covered by email once we have spoken.

Is a broker involved?

No. imisofts is sold by its founder, with no broker. Whether you find it here or on a marketplace such as Acquire.com or Flippa, you deal with the founder from the first email to the handover.
