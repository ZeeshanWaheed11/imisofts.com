Why does intake speed decide which firm gets the case?

Because someone with a legal problem is anxious, and anxious people keep calling until a human being responds. The firm that answers is very often the firm that signs them.

The gap between best and typical is wide. Top-performing firms respond to online enquiries in under five minutes, while the industry median sits around 13 minutes. Broader research finds 78 percent of buyers close with the first responder, and legal enquiries are no exception.

The real problem is not daytime, it is everything else. Accidents, arrests and emergencies do not respect office hours, and a large share of high-value case enquiries arrive at night, at weekends and on holidays. A firm that answers those calls is competing against voicemail. An agent captures the facts while they are fresh, screens against your case criteria so you are not booking consultations you will decline, and books the attorney only when the matter is genuinely in scope.

It captures the intake record, flags conflict-check information for a human to review, and hands anything requiring judgement to a person. It never gives legal advice.
