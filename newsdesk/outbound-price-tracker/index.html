How to read this table

Each row is one dated change that we reported in a story. Date is the day the source announced, decided or published it. Key date is the day it starts or the next deadline, when the source gave one.

Prices are the list prices the vendor published. Your contract may differ.

How rows get here

A row is added when we publish a story about the change. It links the page that owns the fact and our story. Rules and prices move after a row is written, so check the source before you act.
